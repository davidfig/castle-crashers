# Agent notes

- **Never use `git stash`** (including `git stash -u`, `pop`, `drop`). Multiple agents often work on the same branch and working tree at once, so stashing sweeps up, hides, or loses other agents' in-progress changes. To inspect a clean state, use `git diff`/`git show` or a worktree instead.
- **Mobs enter and leave the play area the same way.** Arriving and leaving share one path: over the top = over the hill (flag 2 "entering", drawn climbing behind the crest and down the near slope; exit only once fully hidden, `TOP_EXIT_DEPTH`), over the bottom = behind the foreground ridge. Any new way for a mob to leave (retreat, surrender, scripted exit) must reuse this, mirrored from the arrival, never just vanish at the edge. See `docs/03-gameplay-combat.md` (Over the hill).
- **Test the game muted.** When launching the game in a browser to test or screenshot it, add `?mute=1` to the URL (combine with other dev params, e.g. `?mute=1&seed=5&bots=3`). It keeps sound and music off for that page load without changing the player's remembered N / M toggles.
- **Enemies are generated per run.** `MOBS[type]` is a run's random cast (`src/data/bestiary`, `docs/15-random-bestiary.md`), not the hand-made roster; those live on as `CLASSIC_MOBS` (the sim tests run against them, `?mobs=classic` plays them). Tune a *kind* of monster through `data/bestiary/traits.ts` / `plan.ts` and check with `BESTIARY=<seed> node tools/balance.mjs`; never index monsters by name.
- **Scenery is generated per run, and biomes turn along the road.** `BIOMES` (`src/data/biomes.ts`) are archetypes; a run's biomes come from `generateWorld` (`src/data/scenery`) and are painted into the atlas by `render/sceneryArt.ts` (`docs/17-generated-scenery.md`). Never draw or key sprites by an archetype's bare name: keys are `slot:name`, and a new strip, floor or decal needs a maker (the tests say which). Where one biome gives way to the next is the `Road`'s business, not the level's; look at any stretch with `?seed=1&levels=15&roadx=N` and at a biome's art with `node tools/scenery-sheet.mjs`.
