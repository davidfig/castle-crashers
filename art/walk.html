The whole run as the game walks it: one sim on the seed's one road, from the starting town through every region, camp, road scene and crossroads to the destination, with every monster removed. The hero walks on by itself, stops at each road scene until its conversation has played (skip stop / s walks on), lingers at camps, and steers onto the road crossroads asks for.
